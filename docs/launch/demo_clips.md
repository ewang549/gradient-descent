# Demo clips

Four screen recordings, about 10 minutes for all of them. Each URL opens straight into the state you want.

**Setup, once:** Chrome, a fresh window resized to 1280x800 (or the Window Resizer extension), bookmarks bar hidden,
zoom 100%. Record with Cmd+Shift+5 → "Record Selected Portion", drawn tight around the browser's page area (no tabs,
no address bar). Move the mouse slowly; every clip is 8-15 seconds. Trim in QuickTime (Cmd+T) and post as MP4, not
GIF: X compresses GIFs much harder.

## 1. The calculator flips the call (12 s)

Own 40, 4th & 3, up 4 with 10:00 left in the 4th. A 10-point underdog should go for it. Make them the favorite and
the call flips to punt.

URL: https://your-app.vercel.app/calculator?yl=60&togo=3&diff=4&q=4&t=600&to=3&dto=3&spr=-10&v=h

1. Load the page and wait for the call to read **GO FOR IT** (+1.5 over punting).
2. Scroll down until the **Point spread** slider and "The call" box are both on screen.
3. Start recording. Drag the spread slider slowly from -10 to the right. The call flips to **PUNT** between -7 and
   -6.5 and stays punt all the way to +14.
4. Stop at about +3 and hold for 2 seconds.

Post text idea: "Same spot, same score. A big underdog up 4 should keep attacking; a favorite should punt."

## 2. "You Make the Call" reveal (15 s)

URL: https://your-app.vercel.app/play

1. Start recording on the landing page.
2. Click **Play 10** (random spots, so you can retake until you get one where the coach and the model disagree).
3. Pause a beat on the situation, then press **G**, **P** or **F** (or click).
4. Hold on the reveal for 3-4 seconds: the win probability of every option, what the coach did, what happened.

Use Play 10, not the daily, so recording doesn't use up your daily score.

## 3. A game's win probability chart (12 s)

CIN 27 at PIT 30, 2026 week 3. The lead changes hands 4 times, and Cincinnati gives away 13 WP points on 6 graded 4th
downs, including a 4th & 3 punt in the 2nd quarter (-6.2).

URL: https://your-app.vercel.app/games/2026/2026_03_CIN_PIT

1. Start recording with the chart in view (scroll so the title "PIT WIN PROBABILITY" is near the top).
2. Hover from left to right along the line slowly. The tooltip shows the clock, score and PIT's win probability.
3. Stop on the large red **P** in the 2nd quarter and click it. Hold on the model's numbers for that punt for
   3 seconds.
